\section{How to use keecas in a Quarto
document}\label{how-to-use-keecas-in-a-quarto-document}

This example demonstrates all the key features of \texttt{keecas} for
symbolic and units-aware calculations in a Quarto document.

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced jigsaw, coltitle=black, toprule=.15mm, colback=white, rightrule=.15mm, colbacktitle=quarto-callout-note-color!10!white, leftrule=.75mm, colframe=quarto-callout-note-color-frame, toptitle=1mm, bottomtitle=1mm, titlerule=0mm, opacitybacktitle=0.6, arc=.35mm, opacityback=0, breakable, bottomrule=.15mm, title=\textcolor{quarto-callout-note-color}{\faInfo}\hspace{0.5em}{Note}, left=2mm]

If the rendering engine is \texttt{KaTeX} (i.e.~Jupyter notebook), the
label command \texttt{\textbackslash{}label\{\}} will result in a
\texttt{ParseError}. The latex code will work when rendering by
\texttt{quarto\ render}, but since you may want to see the result before
rendering the document, an option to disable the
\texttt{\textbackslash{}label\{\}} command is provided.

When rendering with \texttt{quarto} you can have two options:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  if \texttt{qmd} files, then everything is fine
\item
  if \texttt{ipynb} files, then pass the \texttt{-\/-execute} flag to
  \texttt{quarto\ render} to rerender the notebook
\end{itemize}

\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Basic Symbolic Math with
Units}\label{basic-symbolic-math-with-units}

Define symbols and calculate basic engineering quantities:

\begin{align}
F & =\mathtt{\text{10.00 kilonewton}} &   & \quad(\mathtt{\text{applied force}})  \label{eq-QUARTO_EXAMPLE-F}  \\[8pt]
 A & =\mathtt{\text{50.00 cm²}} &   & \quad(\mathtt{\text{cross-sectional area}})  \label{eq-QUARTO_EXAMPLE-A}  \\[8pt]
 \sigma & =\dfrac{F}{A} & =2.00{\,}\text{MPa} & \quad(\mathtt{\text{normal stress}})  \label{eq-QUARTO_EXAMPLE-sigma} 
\end{align}

\subsection{Pipe Commands Demonstration}\label{sec-pipe}

Using pipe operators for functional composition:

\begin{align}
x & \mathtt{\text{3.00 m}} &    \\[8pt]
 y & \mathtt{\text{4.00 m}} &    \\[8pt]
 d & =x^{2} + y^{2} & =25.0{\,}\text{m}^{2} 
\end{align}

\subsection{Verification Function}\label{verification-function}

Using the \texttt{check} function for design checks:

\begin{align}
\sigma_{Sd} & \mathtt{\text{20.00 MPa}} &    \\[8pt]
 \tau_{Sd} & \mathtt{\text{15.00 MPa}} &    \\[8pt]
 \sigma_{Rd} & \mathtt{\text{250.00 MPa}} &    \\[8pt]
 \tau_{Rd} & \mathtt{\text{75.00 MPa}} &    \\[8pt]
 \dfrac{\sigma_{Sd}}{\sigma_{Rd}} & =0.080 & \qquad\textcolor{green}{\left[\le1.0\quad \textbf{VERIFIED}\right]}  \\[8pt]
 \dfrac{\tau_{Sd}}{\tau_{Rd}} & =0.200 & \qquad\textcolor{green}{\left[\le1.0\quad \textbf{VERIFIED}\right]} 
\end{align}

The type of comparison in the \texttt{check} function can be specified,
as well the value to be specified against.

\begin{align}
a & =3 & &\textcolor{red}{\left[>1\quad \textbf{NOT VERIFIED}\right]}  \\[8pt]
 b & =4 & &\textcolor{green}{\left[>2\quad \textbf{VERIFIED}\right]}  \\[8pt]
 c & =5 & &\textcolor{red}{\left[\ge3\quad \textbf{NOT VERIFIED}\right]}  \\[8pt]
 d & =6 & &\textcolor{green}{\left[\ge4\quad \textbf{VERIFIED}\right]}  \\[8pt]
 f & =7 & &\textcolor{red}{\left[\neq5\quad \textbf{NOT VERIFIED}\right]} 
\end{align}

\subsection{Subs get ordered}\label{subs-get-ordered}

Substitution will get ordered and sorted automatically, so you can use a
symbol before mapping to a value.

\begin{align}
a & =3 &    \\[8pt]
 b & =4 &    \\[8pt]
 d & =\dfrac{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}}}{c} & =0.417  \\[8pt]
 c & =a{\,}b & =12.000 
\end{align}
